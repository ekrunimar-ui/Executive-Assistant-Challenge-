
# Calendar Conflict Resolution

## Situation

Two high-priority meetings are scheduled at the same time:

1. Investor meeting
2. Key partner meeting

## Approach

Before rescheduling either meeting, I would review:

- Meeting objectives
- Business impact
- Attendees
- Executive priorities
- Flexibility of each meeting
- Previous commitments

## Communication to Key Partner

> Hi [Name],
>
> I apologize for the short-notice request, but an unexpected
> scheduling conflict has come up.
>
> Would you be available to move our meeting to [Option 1]
> or [Option 2]?
>
> We greatly value the opportunity to speak with you and
> appreciate your flexibility.
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]

## Communication to Executive

> Good morning. I noticed that your investor meeting and key
> partner meeting are scheduled for the same time.
>
> I recommend reviewing the priority and flexibility of both
> meetings before moving either one. I will contact the relevant
> party and provide alternative times once confirmed.

## Bonus: Executive Alignment

If the executive later expressed frustration that I rescheduled
the wrong meeting, I would acknowledge the mistake and use it
as an opportunity to better understand their preferences.

I would create an Executive Meeting Priority Matrix identifying
which meetings should generally be protected and which meetings
can be rescheduled.
